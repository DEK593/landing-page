Our Beautiful Wildlife

A fish is a cold-blooded animal with a backbone that lives and breathes underwater using gills.

The koala is a fluffy, tree-dwelling Australian marsupial that eats eucalyptus leaves and sleeps most of the day.

The wolf is a large, wild canine that lives in packs and is known for its thick fur and powerful howl.

Nature is our biggest home, a special place where every tree and animal lives in perfect harmony. If we learn to listen to the silence of the woods and the sound of the wind, we can discover wonderful secrets.
